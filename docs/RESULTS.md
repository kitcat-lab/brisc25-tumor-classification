# Evidence and interpretation

## Canonical versus historical

`results/verified/*_predictions.csv` contains image IDs and four probability columns in the declared class order. These are the primary inputs for corrected analysis. The canonical CNN checkpoint replaces an earlier checkpoint without rewriting the earlier `leakage_v3` CSVs. Both executions are retained under different IDs.

`results/corrected/metrics.csv` is regenerated by the active analysis script. Its CIs are class-stratified, conditional on the observed test set and fixed trained model. They do not account for training-seed variability, patient clusters or test-based model selection.

`results/verified/verified_metrics.json` preserves the audit output including ordinary and stratified bootstrap variants. `results/historical/` preserves the upstream files. Historical metrics/figures are evidence of previous reporting and must not be used as corrected tables.

## Statistical family

The original family is the six unique pairwise contrasts among:

1. Canonical three-block CNN.
2. VGG16 softmax.
3. VGG16 + LightGBM, selected post hoc by test accuracy.
4. RadImageNet softmax.

The analysis joins by image ID and verifies identical labels. It reports continuity-corrected asymptotic McNemar, exact McNemar and Holm adjustment of the six asymptotic p-values. The latter matches the manuscript's stated test family. Exact and asymptotic p-values must not be mixed without defining a new family.

The three public contrasts involving RadImageNet do not reproduce from the final individual predictions; the updated matrix is in `results/corrected/mcnemar.csv`. All remain significant. The VGG16/hybrid result is reproduced exactly and remains nonsignificant. Nonsignificance is not proof of equivalence.

## Classical model

The upstream classical summary reports 90.71%. The audit's Python LightGBM refit reports 90.56% on the same filename/label test cohort. It is separately named `classical_refit`; it must not silently stand in for the originally reported execution. The exploratory refit comparisons use their own four-contrast family and are retained in `results/verified/classical_refit_exploratory_comparisons.csv`.
